Questions & limits

How to use

Choose website, text or Wi-Fi, enter the content, then create your code. Use PNG in messages and documents, or SVG for resizing and printing. Test it with a real phone camera.

Questions & limits

Static QR codes do not expire, but their content cannot be edited later. Linked sites must remain available. Create a new code when details change. Keep the four-module white margin and do not crop the code.

Privacy & use

Files and input stay in your browser. File contents, file names and QR values are not uploaded or sent as analytics events. Google Analytics, Tag Manager, AdSense and Cloudflare may receive ordinary connection data for statistics and advertising.

Keep your originals and check the result before using important documents or printing QR codes. Contact NaNa Lab
